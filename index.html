01 / THE CLUB

BUILT ON VALOR

Valoric FC is a football club based in Halishahar, Chattogram.

Founded by Tahsin #10, the club was created around a simple idea: build a team that plays with passion, unity and courage.

With 40+ players connected to the club, Valoric FC is growing into a football community where every player has a role.

Our identity is simple: POWERED BY VALOR.
